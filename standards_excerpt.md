# Standards Excerpt Pack (Redacted Summary)
Source: Instructor bundle — used as reference context, not for direct RAG lookup by item id.

## Excerpt A: ASME Y14.5-2018 (GD&T Principles)
- **Rule #1 (Envelope Principle):** A feature at MMC must have a perfect form boundary, unless a separate flatness tolerance is specified.
- **Basic Dimensions:** Theoretical, tolerance-free values must be boxed and controlled by a Feature Control Frame.
- **Datum References:** Datums in an FCF must be ordered Primary, Secondary, Tertiary.

## Excerpt B: ISO 128 / ISO 129-1 (General Principles & Dimensioning)
- **Projection Symbols:** The projection cone symbol must sit near the title block to indicate 1st angle (Europe) or 3rd angle (US) system.
- **Dimension Line Rules:** Dimension lines should not cross other extension lines; shorter dimensions nest closer to the part, longer ones sit outside.
- **Leader Lines:** Note leader lines should be angled (30°, 45°, or 60°), never purely vertical or horizontal.
